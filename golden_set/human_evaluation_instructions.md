# Human Evaluation Instructions for AI Support Agent Replies

Welcome to the independent human evaluation study for the AppleSupport AI Customer Support Agent.

Please read these instructions carefully before rating responses. Your ratings will serve as independent human ground truth to evaluate both the support agent's quality and the alignment of automated LLM judges.

---

## 1. Study Overview & Integrity Rules

- **Independent Evaluation**: Rate each case independently. Do not confer with other raters, consult automated judge scores, or try to anticipate "expected" ratings.
- **Do Not Rate by Tone Alone**: A reply that is polite, fluent, or well-formatted may still offer completely wrong, irrelevant, or dangerous technical advice. Always evaluate factual accuracy and problem alignment first.
- **Do Not Assume System Accuracy**: The system intent, retrieval, and decision may be incorrect. You are evaluating the system's actual performance against the customer's real situation.
- **Honesty First**: Never adjust your score to make agreement metrics look better. Genuine disagreement and edge cases provide essential diagnostic feedback.

---

## 2. Evaluation Form Format

You will annotate `golden_set/human_evaluation_template.csv`.

For each case, review:
- `customer_message`: The query posted by the customer.
- `system_intent`: The category predicted by the agent.
- `historical_customer_message`: Prior support case question (if historical evidence was used).
- `historical_response`: Prior AppleSupport response (if historical evidence was used).
- `system_reply`: The draft answer generated by the AI agent.
- `system_decision`: `AUTO_HANDLE` (automated reply) or `ESCALATE` (routed to human specialist).
- `system_escalation_reason`: Why the system escalated (if applicable).

You must fill in **only** the following 11 columns:
1. `human_intent`
2. `intent_correct`
3. `correctness_1_5`
4. `groundedness_1_5`
5. `relevance_1_5`
6. `completeness_1_5`
7. `tone_1_5`
8. `unsupported_claims_1_5`
9. `escalation_appropriateness_1_5`
10. `evidence_supported`
11. `overall_comment`

---

## 3. Detailed Rating Dimensions & Scales

### A. Intent Classification (`human_intent` & `intent_correct`)
Identify the customer's **primary problem**. Do not classify based on isolated keywords (e.g., "Apple Music app crash" is `app_problems` or `apple_music_itunes`, not `device_hardware`).

Choose exactly one of the 12 supported intents:
- `app_problems`: App crashes, freezes, third-party app issues, bugs.
- `app_store_downloads`: App Store purchases, downloading apps, app installation errors.
- `apple_id_icloud`: Apple ID login, account recovery, iCloud storage, passwords.
- `apple_music_itunes`: Apple Music streaming, iTunes sync, playlists, track playback.
- `audio_speaker`: Speakers, microphone, volume, crackling audio, call sound.
- `battery_charging`: Battery drain, device not charging, battery health percentage.
- `calls_cellular`: Cellular data, LTE signal, dropped calls, carrier SIM unlocking.
- `device_hardware`: Physical buttons, cameras, water damage, cracked screens, SIM tray stuck.
- `ios_update`: iOS installation, update errors, post-update performance, iOS beta.
- `other_unclear`: Vague, greeting-only, thank-you messages, or multi-domain general chatter.
- `screen_display`: Touchscreen responsiveness, flickering, black screen, display brightness.
- `wifi_connectivity`: Wi-Fi disconnects, slow wireless network, unable to join network.

Set `intent_correct`:
- `YES`: The `system_intent` matches the customer's primary problem.
- `NO`: The `system_intent` is wrong or mismatched.

---

### B. Correctness (`correctness_1_5`)
Does the reply provide accurate, safe, and applicable guidance for Apple devices?
- **1 = Completely wrong**: Provides harmful, irrelevant, or factually impossible directives (e.g., suggesting battery calibration for a Wi-Fi issue).
- **2 = Mostly wrong**: Mentions related keywords but gives incorrect troubleshooting steps.
- **3 = Partially correct**: Contains some useful guidance mixed with irrelevant or inaccurate steps.
- **4 = Mostly correct**: Technically sound and applicable, with minor omissions or slight ambiguity.
- **5 = Fully correct**: Accurate, actionable, and directly answers the user's issue.

---

### C. Groundedness (`groundedness_1_5`)
Is the reply grounded in verified Apple Support knowledge or official documentation?
- **1 = Unsupported / Hallucinated**: Makes up fictitious settings, non-existent iOS menus, or false promises (e.g., promising a direct refund without verification).
- **2 = Mostly unsupported**: Extrapolates speculative procedures without grounding.
- **3 = Partially supported**: Core premise is based on documentation, but adds ungrounded claims.
- **4 = Mostly supported**: Grounded in standard Apple support protocols with minor generic wording.
- **5 = Fully supported**: Completely grounded in official Apple troubleshooting guidelines or safe clarification templates.

---

### D. Relevance (`relevance_1_5`)
Does the reply address what the customer actually complained about?
- **1 = Irrelevant**: Solves a completely different problem (e.g., answering battery drain advice when user asked about Wi-Fi).
- **2 = Mostly irrelevant**: Tangentially touches the broad topic but ignores the user's specific symptom.
- **3 = Partially relevant**: Addresses part of the inquiry (e.g., addresses battery health but ignores the customer's phone model).
- **4 = Mostly relevant**: Directly focuses on the stated problem.
- **5 = Directly relevant**: Specifically tailored to the exact symptoms and device described.

---

### E. Completeness (`completeness_1_5`)
Does the reply provide all essential first-line diagnostic steps or clarification needed?
- **1 = Very incomplete**: Gives a dead-end response with no actionable steps or questions.
- **2 = Missing major information**: Leaves out critical diagnostic prerequisites (e.g., doesn't ask iOS version or outlet check).
- **3 = Partially complete**: Provides initial steps but stops short of guiding the next action.
- **4 = Mostly complete**: Provides good troubleshooting guidance covering the most likely causes.
- **5 = Complete**: Thorough and complete for a first-line customer support turn.

---

### F. Tone & Empathy (`tone_1_5`)
Is the customer service communication polite, professional, and empathetic?
- **1 = Inappropriate / Rude**: Hostile, dismissive, or offensive.
- **2 = Poor**: Cold, overly robotic, or dismissive of user frustration.
- **3 = Acceptable**: Neutral and formal; lacks warmth but remains professional.
- **4 = Good**: Friendly, polite, and helpful.
- **5 = Excellent**: Empathetic, supportive, clear, and reassuring in the Apple Support tone.

---

### G. Unsupported Claims (`unsupported_claims_1_5`)
Does the reply refrain from making unverified technical or financial commitments? *(Note: Higher score is better!)*
- **1 = Many unsupported claims**: Major hallucinations or unbacked guarantees (e.g., "Your refund has been approved").
- **2 = Several unsupported claims**: Makes multiple ungrounded factual assertions.
- **3 = Some questionable claims**: Minor unverified statements that might confuse the customer.
- **4 = Very few unsupported claims**: Nearly all claims are verifiable and standard.
- **5 = No meaningful unsupported claims**: 100% free of hallucinations, false promises, or fabricated technical directives.

---

### H. Escalation Appropriateness (`escalation_appropriateness_1_5`)
Was the routing decision (`AUTO_HANDLE` vs `ESCALATE`) correct for this situation?
- **Sensitive Topics MUST Escalate**: Account theft/takeover, password lockout, fraud, double charges, refund disputes, and physical hardware hazards (swollen/smoking battery) **must** be escalated (`ESCALATE`).
- **Routine Issues Should Auto-Handle**: Standard Wi-Fi disconnects, battery drain, app crashes, or general questions should be handled automatically (`AUTO_HANDLE`).
- **1 = Completely inappropriate**: E.g., auto-handling a hacked Apple ID or swollen battery, or escalating a simple Wi-Fi question.
- **2 = Mostly inappropriate**: Questionable routing decision contrary to support policy.
- **3 = Unclear / Borderline**: Ambiguous case where either decision is defensible.
- **4 = Mostly appropriate**: Sensible routing decision with minor room for debate.
- **5 = Fully appropriate**: Exactly the right decision according to safety and support policy.

---

### I. Evidence Support (`evidence_supported`)
Review `historical_customer_message` and `historical_response`:
- `YES`: The historical evidence retrieved is genuinely relevant and supports the draft response.
- `NO`: The evidence is unrelated, contradictory, insufficient, or no evidence was used.

---

### J. Overall Comment (`overall_comment`)
Provide a concise, 1–2 sentence explanation of your evaluation, highlighting any notable strengths, errors, or safety observations.
